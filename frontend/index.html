How it works

1. Hold a Binance Agent ID (name.ai) in your wallet.
2. Mint your soulbound Pioneer Badge — free, you only pay gas (~$0.005).
3. Check in once per UTC day. Consecutive days grow your streak; a missed day resets it to 1.
4. The badge can never be transferred or approved to anyone. No owner, no admin, no upgrade — the contract is immutable.
